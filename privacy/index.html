Privacy Policy

Tea Pose · Last updated October 18, 2026

Tea Pose is built so your photos and answers stay on your phone. There is no account, no login and no tracking. This policy explains the little data that does leave your device, and why.

Who we are

Tea Pose is published by Valerio Qunaj. For anything about your data, email apps@quv-studio.com.

What stays on your device

  • Camera and photos. The camera, pose detection and pose matching run entirely on your iPhone using Apple's on-device frameworks. Photos you take, edit or import are never uploaded to us.
  • Your answers and poses. Your name, onboarding answers, personal pose set, favorites and custom poses are stored only on your device.
  • Settings. Preferences like language and auto-shoot sensitivity are stored only on your device.

Deleting the app deletes all of this.

What leaves your device: purchases

Subscriptions are sold and charged by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID, payment details or address.

To check whether you have Tea Pose Pro, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random, anonymous app user ID created by the app (not linked to your name or Apple ID),
  • your purchase and subscription history for Tea Pose (product, price, dates, trial and renewal status), and the App Store receipt,
  • basic technical data such as app version, device model, iOS version, country/storefront and IP address.

This is used only to unlock Pro, restore purchases, handle refunds and cancellations, and see aggregate revenue numbers. It is not used for advertising and not sold to anyone. Legal basis (GDPR): performance of the contract you enter by subscribing (Art. 6(1)(b)) and our legitimate interest in running the service (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Notifications

If you start a free trial and allow notifications, the app schedules a local reminder on your device before the trial ends. No push service or server is involved.

Tracking and ads

Tea Pose does not track you across apps or websites, does not show ads and does not use advertising identifiers.

Children

Tea Pose is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA) you can ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, and object to processing. Since we hold no personal profile, most data can be deleted by deleting the app. For purchase data held by RevenueCat, email us and include the anonymous user ID if you can; we will handle the request within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

To manage or cancel a subscription, use Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions on your iPhone, or Settings › Manage subscription in the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
